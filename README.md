Exactly one word: the first word that comes to mind, no topic, no explanation. Top seven share the $5 pot.

## Why this line

The brief rewards surprise, vividness and a spark of wit over safe filler. 'Petrichor' — the smell of rain on dry earth — is a single vivid sensory word most people have felt but few can name, which is the whole point of a first-thought word.
